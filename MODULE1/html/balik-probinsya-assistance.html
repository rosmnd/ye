WHY YOU MAY NEED IT

A fresh start in the province

Many Pasigueños dream of going back to their home province to be with family and start anew. CSWDO Balik Probinsya Assistance helps make that move possible.

Travel assistance

Transportation support for you and your family returning home.

Resettlement

Help with relocation needs as you resettle in the province.

Family reunification

Be reunited with loved ones and begin a new chapter.

WHO CAN APPLY & WHAT TO PREPARE

Eligibility and requirements

Who can apply

  • Bonafide resident of Pasig City
  • Intends to return or relocate to a home province
  • Low-income or displaced family
  • With a receiving province, LGU, or family
  • Has a registered CSWDO MIS account

Requirements to prepare

  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency in Pasig
  • Letter of Intent to return to the province
  • Proof of destination or acceptance from the receiving LGU or family
  • List of accompanying family members (if any)
  • Accomplished CSWDO application form
ℹ️

Reminder: Coordinate with the CSWDO so your travel and relocation can be properly scheduled and supported.

HOW TO APPLY

Steps to request this assistance

01

Create an account

Register for a CSWDO MIS account or sign in to get started.

02

Choose Balik Probinsya

Select the service and complete the request form with your details.

03

Prepare & attach requirements

Gather the documents listed above and attach or submit them.

04

Wait for verification

The CSWDO team will assess your request and notify you for the release or next steps.
