WHY YOU MAY NEED IT

When medical costs get too heavy

Sickness can happen to anyone. When a hospital stay or a maintenance medicine becomes too expensive, CSWDO Medical Assistance helps ease the burden so you can focus on getting well.

WHO CAN APPLY & WHAT TO PREPARE

Eligibility and requirements

Who can apply

  • Bonafide resident of Pasig City
  • Indigent or financially incapable, as assessed by CSWDO
  • With a verified medical need or condition
  • Filipino citizen
  • Has a registered CSWDO MIS account

Requirements to prepare

  • Valid government-issued ID (Pasig Pass, UMID, PhilSys, or passport)
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency or Indigency
  • Medical Certificate or Doctor's prescription
  • Hospital bill or Statement of Account
  • Laboratory, imaging, or diagnostic request (if applicable)
ℹ️

Reminder: Prepare original or certified true copies of your documents. Requirements may vary per case, and the CSWDO may ask for additional documents during assessment.

HOW TO APPLY

Steps to request this assistance

01

Create an account

Register for a CSWDO MIS account or sign in to get started.

02

Choose Medical Assistance

Select the service and complete the request form with your details.

03

Prepare & attach requirements

Gather the documents listed above and attach or submit them.

04

Wait for verification

The CSWDO team will assess your request and notify you for the release or next steps.
