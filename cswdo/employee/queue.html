6-Step System Flowchart (Queue Management Workflow)

Step 1: Client Arrival & Control No. Input

Online & Walk-In clients present their Control Number and physical requirements. Staff inputs Control Number and system tracks total user counts.

Step 2: Validation & OL/WI ID Generation

Staff validates attachments against physical hard copies and sets status to "for Interview", generating OL-2026-MMDD-Seq or WI-2026-MMDD-Seq ordered by Control Number.

Step 3: Live Queueing & Calling to Interview

Public TV displays OL/WI IDs with Control Numbers. Clicking "Call Next" & "Start Interview" logs Date, Interviewer, and Time Interview for the Intake Sheet.

Step 4: Post-Interview & Admin Trigger

After the interview assessment, the module updates the Admin status view and sends an Alert, Notification, and Message to the Admin for evaluation.

Step 5: Admin Approval & Staff Toast

Admin reviews the record and clicks Approve. The system immediately dispatches a real-time notification back to the staff and sets status to Approved.

Step 6: Report Release & Survey Hand-off

Staff generates the signable report and clicks "Release Client", logging Time Released and passing the Control Number to the Customer Satisfaction Survey.
