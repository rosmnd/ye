WHY YOU MAY NEED IT

Help during a difficult time

Losing a loved one is hard enough without the worry of funeral costs. CSWDO Burial Assistance helps indigent families in Pasig give their loved one a dignified send-off.

🕊️

Funeral expenses

Help with funeral service and related costs.

⚰️

Interment

Support for casket, interment, and burial fees.

💛

Bereavement support

Relief for grieving families who cannot afford burial costs.

WHO CAN APPLY & WHAT TO PREPARE

Eligibility and requirements

Who can apply

  • Bonafide resident of Pasig City
  • The deceased is an indigent Pasig resident
  • Claimant is a relative or authorized person
  • Has a registered CSWDO MIS account

Requirements to prepare

  • Death Certificate (PSA or Local Civil Registrar)
  • Barangay Certificate of Indigency
  • Funeral contract or Statement of Account
  • Proof of relationship of the claimant to the deceased
  • Valid government-issued ID of the claimant
  • Permit to bury or burial documents (if applicable)
ℹ️

Reminder: Apply as soon as possible after the death of your loved one so the assistance can be processed quickly.

HOW TO APPLY

Steps to request this assistance

01

Create an account

Register for a CSWDO MIS account or sign in to get started.

02

Choose Burial Assistance

Select the service and complete the request form with your details.

03

Prepare & attach requirements

Gather the documents listed above and attach or submit them.

04

Wait for verification

The CSWDO team will assess your request and notify you for the release or next steps.
