Late Check-in Your appointment has been marked as LATE. You may be re-queued if capacity is still available; if capacity is full, you will need to reschedule.

Check-in opens at 7:30 AM for AM sessions (valid until before 11:00 AM) and 11:30 AM for PM sessions (valid until before 5:00 PM). Arriving past the check-in window marks your appointment as LATE. Missed AM sessions may be re-queued to the PM session subject to slot capacity, or rescheduled. Missed PM sessions must be rescheduled for the next working day. Re-queued clients receive a new queue number and must wait at the end of the line. The temporary queue number is issued only after check-in.
